## Project: Novellia Pets
Novellia Pets is a new service where pet owners can track and manage their pet's medical
records. You'll be building the MVP.
We've intentionally left some decisions open. Part of what we're evaluating is how you navigate
ambiguity, what questions you ask, what tradeoffs you make, and how clearly you can articulate
your reasoning. There's no single right answer here.
## What to build
Build an app where a user can manage their pets and their pets' medical records. At minimum
this should include:
- Adding, viewing, editing, and deleting pets
- Adding, viewing, editing, and deleting medical records for a pet
- A dashboard or overview that gives a sense of the state of all pets
- Some way to find or filter pets or records
- One feature you think would make this genuinely useful or interesting. We care more about why you chose it than what it is.
We'll leave the specifics of your data model largely up to you. Be prepared to explain your choices.
## A few things to keep in mind
- You don't need to implement authentication, but be ready to discuss how you'd approach
it
- Use whatever persistence layer makes sense to you. It just needs to survive a page
refresh.
- During the review session, we'll ask you to pair on adding a new feature or two.
## What we're not looking for
Don't worry about scalability, DevOps, or production hardening. We're interested in how you
think, how you structure your work, and how you communicate your decisions.
Submission
Include a Loom walkthrough (under 10 minutes) covering:
- A demo of the app
- A walkthrough of your key architecture and design decisions
- One decision you made that you're not fully satisfied with, and what you'd do differently
with more time
Ensure your solution is documented and runnable with minimal setup.
